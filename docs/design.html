Do's and Don'ts

Do
  • Preserve the camera and table proportions.
  • Keep phase, odds, and wallet state visible.
  • Scale one Canvas coordinate system.
  • Animate dealing from the live reveal clock.
Don't
  • Copy the recorded dealer's identity.
  • Invent unsettled side bets.
  • Use flickering or moving hit regions.
  • Let the UI obscure the dealer or table.
